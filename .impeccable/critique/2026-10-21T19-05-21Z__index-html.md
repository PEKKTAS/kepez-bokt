### Key Fixes Implemented:
1. **Instant Preloader Unlock:** Reduced initial blocking wait from 181 frames (102 MB) down to a 10-frame critical buffer (~5 MB), unlocking the site in ~1.2s.
2. **Progressive Background Streaming:** Non-critical frames stream in batches of 6 via `requestIdleCallback`, eliminating UI freeze.
3. **Nearest-Frame Fallback:** Eliminates canvas blank flickers if rapid scrubbing occurs before all frames arrive.
